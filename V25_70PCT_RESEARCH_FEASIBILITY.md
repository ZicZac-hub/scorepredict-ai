# V25 Five-Double 70% Accuracy Feasibility Research

**22 November 2026 – original archived score distributions, no changes to live predictions**

## Research question

Can five **joint exact-score** combinations on **two football matches** really
achieve 70% of verified, future five-double results?

**Not demonstrated.** There is no defensible way to promise or configure
70% accuracy when the independently verified V25 research cohort is only
1 winning set from 4 completed pairs (1 pending).

Separately, the original V8 same-pair frozen forecast distributions put
only **5.9%–7.6% modeled probability mass** on their mathematically most
probable five joint scorelines. Between **115 and 156 different joint-score
combinations** would be needed to capture 70% *model-estimated mass* on
those six archived original pairs. This is NOT 70% actual success, and
115–156 predictions would be a different format, not five doubles.

For these original V8 distributions, no alternative selection of only five
cells has higher V8-model probability than the V8 optimal five. V25's
robust picks can differ and might be better on real future results, but
cannot be declared more accurate before independently verified testing.

## New, exact mathematical checks

The report generated by `v25_five_double_frontier.py` now calculates:

- Exact highest-probability coverage for top 1, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100,
  150, 200, 500 and 1,000 distinct **joint** score combinations.
- Minimum number of *distinct two-match exact-score outcomes* required
  to meet a prespecified **70% model-mass target**, or a transparent
  computation-limit status where it cannot be established.
- How much V25's originally frozen robust five combinations cover under
  the same original V8 grid.
- Genuine V8/V25 five-double observed hit rates, confidence intervals
  and pending counts from the independent matched archival result audit.
- Separate V25 coverage only when both probability grids were
  **actually frozen before kickoff** and are independently reproducible.

The calculation uses a sorted Cartesian score grid and a heap to enumerate
the highest-probability cells exactly, without fitting new parameters or
inventing a source. It assumes independent matches conditional on the
saved distributions and explicitly distinguishes model mass from wins.

The website V25 research screen and on-demand five-double generator
display the model frontier *without modifying any selected predictions*.

## Proposed improvement and evidence gate

The next way to pursue a higher observed hit rate is better full-score
**calibration** using authenticated pre-kickoff data, not multiplying scores,
manipulating confidence, betting-market rumours, or changing predictions
after results.

Test future challenger distributions against V8 **on the same fixtures**,
measuring multiclass Brier and log loss, score-tail calibration and actual
five-double hits. The pair-screening policy must be frozen before kickoff,
including all examined candidates, and report abstention frequency alongside
wins. Do not cherry-pick a few won sets.

For a 70% claim, require at least 100 independently verified, frozen
five-double sets and a 95% Wilson lower confidence bound at or above 70%.
Even this is a minimum review gate rather than a guarantee of future wins.

No live model has been promoted and no previously archived score was changed.
No extra provider or AI requests are performed by the new evaluation.
