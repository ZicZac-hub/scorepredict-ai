# V25 score accuracy: read-only goal-distribution research

**22 November 2026, Australia/Sydney. Research-only; live predictions unchanged.**

## Problem and evidence

The independently audited V25 robust five-double sample has **1 win / 4 verified
same-pair sets**, with **1 pending**. V8's matched baseline had **0 / 4**.
These four events do not demonstrate a generalizable model improvement.

The previously frozen V8 scoreline audit found its high-goal event
(at least four total regulation goals) was assigned mean probability
about **0.344** versus about **0.288** observed over 59 verified fixtures.
This is an early diagnostic, not a license to reduce the high-goal mass in
production. Testing must use identical verified fixtures and proper losses.

## What the update calculates

The independent V8/V25 matched audit now attaches a new research section
named v25_score_accuracy_research to its JSON report, tracking:

- Top-one, top-three and top-five **single-match exact-score hits**;
- Exact-score log loss and **full multinomial** score Brier loss;
- Per-team goal mean absolute error and total-goals bias;
- Predicted versus observed 4+ total-goal and 0-1 total-goal rates,
  with each event's Brier loss;
- Fully **same-leg** V8 versus originally frozen V25 probability comparisons,
  with negative V25-minus-V8 loss differences indicating a lower loss;
- A separately marked **retrospective replay** of fixed Poisson,
  negative-binomial, bivariate Poisson, and equal mixture sensitivities
  derived from previously frozen V8 score grids.

A retrospective mathematical transformation is **not** a new pre-kickoff V25
forecast. It must never be included in the independently frozen V25 accuracy
denominator. Only future V25 sets with actual, originally frozen normalized
score grids can enter the genuine prospective probability comparison.

The report does not fit parameters on verified results, make new API requests,
change existing selections, regenerate past forecasts, or promote a model.

## Where to inspect results

- data/accuracy_diagnosis/v8_v25_matched.json: automatically regenerated by
  the existing hourly independently verified results workflow after the change
  reaches the default branch.
- v25_score_accuracy_research.py: reproducible arithmetic for both score
  distributions and the clearly labelled sensitivity replays.
- tests/test_v25_score_accuracy_research.py: proper loss, high-score,
  unsupported-score and no-mutation checks.
- tests/test_v8_v25_matched_audit.py: pending and same-leg audit invariants.

## Promotion criteria

No model weights, V25 robust picks, live score grids, pair selection or
public-facing confidence are changed based on this audit. A proposal to update
the live model first needs independent verification of newly frozen V25 grids
on sufficient matched matches, improvement in exact-score log loss and Brier,
and non-inferior high/low-score calibration. Five-double hit rates must be
reported on genuinely archived same-pair sets, without rewriting selections.

Even a full 100-pair sample may be insufficient to establish a small effect;
report confidence intervals and source age, and treat pending as pending.
There is **no proven 70% five-double win rate**.

## Research basis

- Dixon & Coles (1997), *Modelling Association Football Scores and
  Inefficiencies in the Football Betting Market*, DOI:
  https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9876.00065
- Wheatcroft (2021), *Evaluating probabilistic forecasts of football
  matches: the case against the ranked probability score*, DOI:
  https://doi.org/10.1515/jqas-2019-0089
- *Predicting Football Match Results Using a Poisson Regression Model*
  (2024), https://doi.org/10.3390/app14167230

These sources support explicit probabilistic score modelling and proper
verification, not a claim that any specific V25 variant is more accurate.
